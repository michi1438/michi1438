"A cult classic" were the words Roger read when he closed the book he had just finished already wishing the day over. The sun was not yet passed zenith, he had just ate a piece of a spicy jerky that faked a certain fullness in his belly.
He was sitting on the mossy patch he usualy sat on when on his way to Clayton, the small rural conglomerat of house out of the main hub in a 100km area, Segwin, where Roger lives. He had a meeting with Janice, unwillingly, he got up, put his bag on his shoulder, and started on the small 30 minute walk left towards Clayton, he had already walked more than an hour earlier that morning...

Janice was a friend of a friend, and therefore mostly a pro-bono case for Roger, the man she lived with had not come back from a fishing trip he had set on 3 days ago, and there were good reasons to believe he'd got killed, not leaving any trace, like most premeditated murders, cleaners do a pretty good job in these regions. Fred, Janice's partner, was mostly a fisher in the delta surrounding Clayton, mostly crawfish and crabs, he was also known to be a fearless gambler. He had won big around one table a week or so ago in the basement of a shady swamper. Gambling has always been a risky hobby, winning big even more so... But the gains in them self were not the only thing that could have led to his death. He had bought a new boat made by a Ludwig, a famous familly around Segwin for their craftmanship, famous for their loose tongues as well. So Fred showing up with a big pouch of high capacity chips, from back when Altoids was manufacturing, to buy his new fancy boat at the cost of 7 of those chips could just as well be the reason his body, was not, still floating and visible to all... 
Altoids was one of the few manufactures that had acheived this level of industrial process, their high capacity chips had become the standard value on which most, if not all, trades where based. Intrestingly their recent rarity had made them more valuable then the base they still represented, in other words Joseph Ludwig had priced his boat at 12 chips when first asked, but as he saw the actual chips in Fred's bag, beyond droping his jaw in surprise, he only asked for 8 of them in exchange of the boat, if Fred'd been a shrewd negotiator, Joseph would have traded out the boat for 4.

As Roger was approching the first houses of Clayton, going up one of the multiple muddy banks of the many streams in the area, 2 young thin kid were trying their luck with a brocken ring trap and a small piece of fatty meat in the shallow of the stream, and a bigger group of taller but mostly larger kid were playing kicking some undefined old piece of plastic around trying to steal it from one another and yelling strong prepubescent war cries. Roger did'nt stop, he just noticed the group of mother looking after the youngs, smoking cigarettes and not doing anymore effort to be attractive, it was easy for Roger to assume they had their fill of motherhood just glancing at the field he had passed. That did not keep Roger from his sultry looks as he passed the depraved looking mothers...
