"A cult classic" were the words Roger read when he closed the book he had just finished already wishing the day over. The sun was not yet passed zenith, he had just ate a piece of a spicy jerky that faked a certain fullness in his belly.
He was sitting on the mossy patch he usualy sat on when on his way to Clayton, the small rural conglomerat of house out of the main hub in a 100km area, Segwin, where Roger lives. He had a meeting with Janice, unwillingly, he got up, put his bag on his shoulder, and started on the small 30 minute walk left towards Clayton, he had already walked more than an hour earlier that morning...

Janice was a friend of a friend, and therefore mostly a pro-bono case for Roger, the man she lived with had not come back from a fishing trip he had set on 3 days ago, and there were good reasons to believe he'd got killed, not leaving any trace, like most premeditated murders, cleaners do a pretty good job in these regions. Fred, Janice's partner, was mostly a fisher in the delta surrounding Clayton, mostly crawfish and crabs, he was also known to be a fearless gambler. He had won big around one table a week or so ago in the basement of a shady swamper. Gambling has always been a risky hobby, winning big even more so... But the gains in them self were not the only thing that could have led to his death. He had bought a new boat made by a Ludwig, a famous familly around Segwin for their craftmanship, famous for their loose tongues as well. So Fred showing up with a big pouch of high capacity chips, from back when Altoids was manufacturing, to buy his new fancy boat at the cost of 7 of those chips could just as well be the reason his body, was not, still floating and visible to all... 
Altoids was one of the few manufactures that had acheived this level of industrial process, their high capacity chips had become the standard value on which most, if not all, trades where based. Intrestingly their recent rarity had made them more valuable then the base they still represented, in other words Joseph Ludwig had priced his boat at 12 chips when first asked, but as he saw the actual chips in Fred's bag, beyond droping his jaw in surprise, he only asked for 8 of them in exchange of the boat, if Fred'd been a shrewd negotiator, Joseph would have traded out the boat for 4.

As Roger was approching the first houses of Clayton, going up one of the multiple muddy banks of the many streams in the area, 2 young thin kid were trying their luck with a brocken ring trap and a small piece of fatty meat, in the shallow of the stream, and a bigger group of taller, but mostly larger kid were playing, kicking some undefined old piece of plastic around trying to steal it from one another and yelling strong prepubescent war cries. Roger did'nt stop, he mearly noticed the group of mother looking after the youngs, smoking cigarettes and not doing anymore effort to be attractive, it was easy for Roger to assume they had their fill of motherhood just glancing at the field he had passed. That did not keep Roger from his sultry looks as he passed the depraved looking mothers, not that he would do anything about it...

Soon after he arrived at a small one story house that kind of looked like a mobile-home but "enhanced" with rough wood board extension, a few windows were broken and patched up with various mundane article, a broken mirror, a tape-up piece of plastic and the likes, the floor of the front patio where half broken and still the house felt warming and welcoming, walking the 2 steps to the porche it was clear up until recently the couple had cared deaply about their home. He knocked just out the frame of the door, shy at first, affraid to break something, waited a bit then struck gain with more enthusiasm, someone on the other side ran and opened the door huridly with a half-cautious yet explosive "Hello Mr. Montrose!".

She was beautiful, that's what he thought first, a fraction of a second being scared not to be able to talk or choke. She had big eyes, endearing and soft helped by the still wet eyelashes her hair in a mess that seemed strangely cared for, a slight red hue under her skin, definite over her cheekbones, she had been crying. Roger knew before the door opened, both that she'd be crying and how gorgeous she would be by the general description of their mutual friend, still it was a minor step back in his speech. Barely noticable from the exterior he went on with protocol "Bonjour Janice, you can call me Roger." with the unnoticed or unacknowledge discomfort, "How are you holding up?".
Ignoring his question she invited him in, and showed him to a chair in the kitchen, she turned her back and went to pour a cup of tea and toping her own at the same time, she pulled a piece of lint out from her loosely fitted jeans, maybe not her own, and wiped her eyes.
"I don't know were to start" she whispered as a long sigh. Roger was uncomfortable but with another nudge started:
"There is no good way to process any of this, what I'm here to do is help you with the facts, process the idea and get you on the right track of our next steps in this... mess" He was used to being very pragmatical in those situations, this time he felt cold and had doubts about his logic, he felt weak facing pain on that sweet face, but kept on, "Do you know where you stand, do you want vengeance? Are you scared for yourself? Do you think you know who did it?"
"I'm guessing you are here to advocate against vengeance!" and as she was saying this, the rest of his question registered, "What do you mean scared for myself? Why should I? Do you think they might come for me? He had only left a few of those chips back here with me.."
"Unlikely" he retorted, "I'm just trying to gage the situation, I did'nt mean to scare you. As a word of advise, I would omite saying to anyone that you have chips left, even to me..." He tried a comforting smile, but then realized they had'nt gone through the tought part yet, she tenously smiled back in acknoledgement. "And yes I would try to not have vengeance outright, but we are here to find the best course of action if you'd feel like vengeance then we'd want to give it to you in the cleanest way possible, so as to not lead to any retaliations, do you feel vengful?" He asked again,
"No, I'm lost, I'm not even sure who did it, if somebody did, or even if he is actually... dead..." As she said those last word her eyes sparkled and tears droped.. He was here to early as he often was, you never knew how people reacted especialy the first few days, she was still dening somewhat the death. She was the one that called it in, she knew and yet kept living on the doubt, for now it was easier than admiting it."I'm sure he's dead, it was murder and I know who did it!" He was testing, her reaction would tell him to stay and help or walk out the door not looking back as she screamed bloody murder, hot temper was a dangerous trait, nobody messed with it, turning his back on an angry emotional and possibly armed person was never a good option, but the repercutions of a hot temper was a bigger well known threat in his profession.
